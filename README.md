A one-page newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image. Lead story: a US jury finds Huawei guilty of stealing trade secrets and bank fraud (10 of 12 counts) but clears it of racketeering. Also inside: four tankers hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz, Ukraine's drone strikes on Russian ships in the Gulf of Finland and off Libya, Senegal winning back the Africa Cup of Nations title, China's Shenzhou-24 carrying the first foreign astronaut to Tiangong, Nvidia sliding as US consumer confidence jumps — plus the day's other headlines in a ticker strip.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) draws the whole page with a hand-built 5×7 pixel font and a tiny PNG encoder — no network, no randomness, no clock — so two runs write byte-identical PNGs.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
